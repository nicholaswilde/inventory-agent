## Error Codes & Diagnostics

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Blue Light Ring (Pulsing)` | Electrolyzing in progress; cleaner is being made | Allow conversion cycle to run (~6-8 minutes) until ring turns solid green. |
| `Green Light Ring (Solid)` | Cleaner is ready to use; 14-day potency counter active | Pour solution into spray bottle. Double-click power button to start day counter if keeping plugged in. |
| `Yellow Light (Single Section)` | Cleaner has 2 days remaining before expiration | Use remaining solution or prepare to brew a fresh batch. |
| `Half Red Light Ring` | Electrolyzer fill error, improper capsule dilution, or incorrect power supply | Ensure appliance is filled to line with cool water, full capsule contents added and waited 10 seconds before pressing start; verify official power cord. |
| `Red Light Ring (Solid)` | Cleaner has expired (day 14+) or electrolysis cycle failed | Discard expired solution, rinse electrolyzer, add fresh water and new activator capsule, and restart cycle. |
