## Error Codes & Diagnostics

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Heater Does Not Power On / No Glow` | Power cord unplugged, circuit breaker tripped, or pull switch unengaged | Ensure power cord is connected to a grounded 120V 60Hz outlet; check main fuse/breaker; pull switch cord once to engage. |
| `Sudden Power Cut-Off` | Built-in tip-over safety switch activated or overheat protection triggered | Place heater on a firm, flat, level surface in an upright position; allow unit to cool if thermal cut-off activated. |
| `Reduced Heat Output / Element Flickering` | Voltage drop from undersized extension cord or loose connection | Plug directly into wall outlet or use minimum 14 AWG extension cord rated for >= 1875W. |
| `Surface Wear / Finish Degradation` | Harsh chemical cleaner or abrasive powder used | Disconnect power and cool completely; wipe outer shell with soft damp cloth and mild detergent only. |
