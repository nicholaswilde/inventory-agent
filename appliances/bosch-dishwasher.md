## Common Indicators & Troubleshooting

| Indicator / Symptom | Possible Cause | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Check water supply` indicator on | Water supply shut off or restricted | Check household supply valve under sink; inspect inlet hose for kinks or clogged filter screen. |
| `Sanitized` indicator flashes | Water temperature fell short of sanitization threshold | Run hot water faucet before starting cycle to prime hot water line. |
| `Refill Rinse Aid` indicator | Rinse aid reservoir empty | Refill dispenser; adjust dosage setting (r:01 to r:06) if spotting or streaking occurs. |
| Standing water in bottom of tub | Previous cycle interrupted or drain obstructed | Hold **Start/Reset** button for 3 seconds to cancel and force drain cycle. If water remains, check filter system and pump impeller. |
| Touch keys unresponsive or erratic | Moisture or grease on control fascia | Wipe top edge fascia dry with soft cloth (capacitive sensors detect moisture). Open/close door to reset touch panel. |
| Odor inside tub | Food residue buildup in filter or drain | Clean filter assembly and run Heavy cycle with dishwasher cleaner or citric acid crystals. |

## Care & Maintenance

- **3-Stage Filter System** (Cylindrical Micro Filter, Flat Fine Filter, Coarse Filter):
  - Rotate cylindrical filter counter-clockwise to remove.
  - Rinse under running water with a soft nylon brush every 1–2 months.
  - Reassemble in reverse order and turn clockwise until arrow marks align.
- **Drain Pump Access**:
  - Remove filter system and sponge out standing water.
  - Using a spoon, pry off white drain pump cover until it clicks off.
  - Check impeller for seeds, broken glass, or debris (use caution for sharp objects).
  - Press pump cover back down firmly until it snaps into locked position before replacing filters.
- **Spray Arms**:
  - Unscrew upper spray arm nut to remove; lift lower spray arm straight up.
  - Clear clogged nozzle holes with a toothpick and rinse.
