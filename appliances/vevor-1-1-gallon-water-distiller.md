## Error Codes & Diagnostics

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Undistilled Water Drops Out` | Power supply of host or machine head unplugged; start button not pressed (red light off); or no water in main tank | Ensure both head and host power cords are firmly connected; press start button; fill main engine tank with water. |
| `Amount of Distilled Water Not Enough` | Water does not reach max level scale; poor sealing ring seal or leakage; or outlet nozzle improperly installed | Add water up to max scale line; inspect, clean, and reseat or replace sealing ring; reinstall outlet nozzle properly. |
| `Head Assembly Shaking or Water Overflow` | Water exceeds max level line; head assembly not seated flush; sealing ring not installed properly; or outlet nozzle clogged | Do not overfill past max scale; reseat head firmly onto host machine; reinstall sealing ring correctly; clean or replace outlet nozzle. |
