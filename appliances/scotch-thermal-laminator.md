## Error Codes & Diagnostics

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Pouch Jam / Misfeed` | Pouch jammed or misfed into rollers (often caused by inserting open-end first or cutting pouch before laminating) | Unplug laminator immediately and allow to cool. Use jam release lever on back of machine to gently pull pouch out from entry side. |
| `Hazy / White Blotches` | Lamination pouch appears hazy or has white blotches due to insufficient heat/speed | Ensure laminator is fully heated (Ready light on) and correct mil setting is selected (3 mil vs 5 mil). Re-feed pouch sealed-end first. |
| `Wrinkling / Curling` | Item wrinkled or curled exiting rollers | Ensure item is aligned straight, not thicker than 0.015 in., and allow laminated item to cool flat upon exit. |
