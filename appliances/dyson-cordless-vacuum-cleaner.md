## Error Codes & Diagnostics

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Battery Fault (Flashing Red LED)` | Battery fault detected | Contact Dyson Helpline / replace battery pack. |
| `Charger Fault (Flashing Alt Red/Blue)` | Charger or power connection fault | Check wall socket and charger cable; replace charger if fault persists. |
| `Airway Blockage (Pulsing Motor)` | Airway obstruction or bin full | Empty bin; inspect wand, inlet, and cleaner head for blockages; remove debris. |
| `Filter Alert (Filter LED / Low Suction)` | Filter is dirty, wet, or needs washing | Wash filter under cold water at least once a month; air dry completely for at least 24 hours before reinstalling. |
