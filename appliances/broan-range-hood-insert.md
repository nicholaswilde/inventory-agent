## Error Codes & Diagnostics

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Blower Does Not Turn On` | Power supply disconnected or blower switch faulty | Check service panel circuit breaker; verify wiring connections in switch box; check blower switch (B03295080). |
| `Lights Do Not Illuminate` | Burned out bulb or light switch faulty | Allow bulbs to cool; replace with Max 40W 120V Candelabra base (E12) bulbs; check light switch (B03295081). |
| `Excessive Noise or Vibration` | Loose ducting or blower wheel obstruction | Inspect ductwork for loose dampers or improper transitions; check blower wheel for grease accumulation or debris. |
