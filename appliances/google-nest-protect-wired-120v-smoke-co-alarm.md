## Error Codes & Diagnostics

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Sensors Have Failed (Yellow Light)` | Smoke or carbon monoxide sensor has failed automatic self-test | Clean according to instructions (wipe exterior with damp cloth, clean dust with compressed air or vacuum); if warning persists, replace unit. |
| `Low Battery (Yellow Light)` | Backup battery level is low | Replace with 3 fresh AA Energizer Ultimate Lithium (L91) batteries. Never use standard alkaline or rechargeable batteries. |
| `Nest Protect Has Expired (Yellow Light)` | 10-year internal smoke and CO sensor lifespan has ended | Replace entire Nest Protect unit. |
| `Heads-Up Alert (Yellow Light + Voice)` | Rising levels of smoke or carbon monoxide detected | Check for source of smoke or CO; silence alarm via Nest app or physical button press if non-emergency. |
| `Emergency Alarm (Red Light + Siren + Voice)` | Critical emergency smoke or carbon monoxide level detected | Evacuate immediately to fresh air; call emergency services (911). |
| `Nightly Promise (Green Glow)` | Automatic self-test passed; sensors and backup batteries are working properly | Normal reassurance glow when room lights are turned off. |
