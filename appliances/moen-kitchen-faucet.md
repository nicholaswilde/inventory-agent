## Diagnostics & Troubleshooting

| Symptom / LED Signal | Possible Cause | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|
| Blue light flashes on sensor | Low battery warning | Replace all 6 AA batteries in battery pack under sink. |
| Faucet does not turn on with wave sensor | Batteries dead or disconnected control box | Verify battery orientation and cable connection to control box; check if manual handle is shut off in manual mode. |
| Water turns on unexpectedly | Moisture or reflection near wave sensor | Clean wave sensor lens on faucet neck with soft damp cloth; keep objects clear of detection zone. |
| Low or weak spray pressure | Clogged screen washer in spray wand | Unscrew spray wand from hose, inspect mesh screen washer, rinse sediment under running water, and reassemble. |
| Faucet continues running / won't stop | Control box logic locked up | Unplug battery pack from control box for 60 seconds to perform hard reset, then reconnect. Turn off manual handle. |
| Drips from spout or base | Worn cartridge seals | Replace cartridge with Moen `1255`. Turn off water valves under sink before removing handle. |

## Maintenance & Care

- **Cleaning Finish**: Clean with mild liquid dishwashing soap and warm water; dry with a soft cloth. Never use cleaners containing abrasives, ammonia, bleach, acids, or alcohol.
- **Sensor Lens**: Keep sensor window free of grease, water droplets, and mineral deposits.
- **Batteries**: Standard alkaline AA batteries typically last ~1 year under average household use.
