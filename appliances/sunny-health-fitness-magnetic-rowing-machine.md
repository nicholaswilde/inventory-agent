## Error Codes & Diagnostics

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Computer Display Blank or Faint` | Batteries depleted, missing, or installed incorrectly | Replace with 2 fresh AAA alkaline batteries; check positive/negative terminal orientation. |
| `No Stroke Count / Zero Readings` | Sensor wire disconnected or flywheel magnet misaligned | Check sensor wire (#45-1) connection to computer; inspect flywheel magnet (#51) alignment. |
| `Uneven or Jerky Rowing Resistance` | Tension cable misaligned or mesh belt jammed | Inspect mesh belt wheel (#26) and volute spring (#21); verify tension knob (#14) cable connection. |
| `Squeaking or Rough Seat Glide` | Debris, dust, or worn rollers on sliding rail | Wipe sliding rail (#62) with clean dry cloth; inspect seat rollers and 608Z bearings (#55) for wear. |
| `Sliding Rail Wobble / Instability` | Loose frame bolts or quick-release knob | Tighten M12 knob (#47), pull pin (#60), and front/rear stabilizer screws securely. |
