## Error Codes & Diagnostics

| Code / Display | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `HIGH` | Temperature of oven area too high (>100°F / 38°C) | Allow oven area to cool down before pressing START (normal operating range: 14°F/-10°C to 100°F/38°C). |
| `LOW` | Temperature of oven area too low (<14°F / -10°C) | Move unit to warmer environment before operating. |
| `BREAD PAN` | Bread pan abnormal position / not locked in place | Reinsert bread pan firmly until it clicks into position. Check display and press START. |
| `Flashing Time / BREAD` | Power interruption or STOP button pressed | Check power plug connection; restart cycle with fresh ingredients if power was cut during baking. |
| `Smoke / Burning Odor` | Ingredients spilled on heating element or oven area | Unplug unit, let cool, and thoroughly clean out crumbs or spilled ingredients from oven cavity. |
