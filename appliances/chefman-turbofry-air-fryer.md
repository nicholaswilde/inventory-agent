## Error Codes & Diagnostics

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Unit Does Not Turn On / No Heat` | Basket not pushed completely into housing, power cord unplugged, or timer set to 0 | Ensure basket is pushed fully closed until seated flush; check 120V outlet connection and turn timer knob past 0. |
| `Unevenly Cooked / Underdone Food` | Basket overfilled or food not shaken/flipped halfway through cooking | Cook food in smaller single-layer batches; shake basket or flip food halfway through cooking time. |
| `White Smoke During Cooking` | Excess oil/grease accumulated in bottom of basket from high-fat ingredients | Allow unit to cool; drain and wipe excess grease from bottom of basket; clean basket and tray between batches. |
| `Plastic Odor During Initial Use` | Normal manufacturing protective coating burn-off | Wash basket and tray thoroughly in warm soapy water before first use; run empty at 400°F for 10 minutes. |
| `Nonstick Coating Peeling / Scratches` | Use of metal utensils or abrasive scouring pads | Never use metal tongs or abrasive scouring pads; use heat-resistant silicone/wooden tongs and wash with soft sponge (top-rack dishwasher safe). |
